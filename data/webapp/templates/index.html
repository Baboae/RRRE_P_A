About

Explore your Raceroom ranked career history through detailed statistical insights provided by this tool. The official website features a dashboard displaying your finishing positions, ELO, and other statistics for your last 100 races, along with a career page to review your complete racing history.

Designed to expand upon these graphs, this site helps you better understand your results by uncovering extra data points from the source files that aren't visible on your career page.
